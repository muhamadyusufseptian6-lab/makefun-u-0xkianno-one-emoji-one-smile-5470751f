Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Emoji, One Smile** — one emoji, the one most likely to make a stranger smile.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib-only, deterministic) writes exactly one emoji to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. No words, no caption, no explanation — just the emoji.

## The emoji

🫶 — heart hands. A small wordless gesture: two hands shaping a heart and offering it to whoever is looking.

Why this one, and not the first smiley that comes to mind:

- **Warm at a glance.** No context needed — open hands offering a heart read as kind and friendly the instant you see them.
- **Universal.** No face, no words, no irony, no inside joke; a child, a grandparent, and a stranger on the other side of the world all read it the same way.
- **Chosen, not default.** Thousands of entries will be 🙂, 😊, ❤️ or 😄. Heart hands is a deliberate pick: a gesture, not a face — it says something without copying the obvious.
- **Fine from a stranger.** It is an offering, not an embrace: warm without intimacy, affection without presumption, and nothing a stranger could read as sarcasm or flirtation.

One gesture, one smile — handed over, not asked for.

## Run locally

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/one-emoji-out node main.ts
cat /tmp/one-emoji-out/entry.txt
```
